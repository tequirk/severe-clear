# User Guide

## Overview

Say where you're starting, where you're going, and when you're leaving, and see the weather you may experience along the way.

## Planning a Trip

When you open the app, you will be asked to provide where you're starting, where you're going, and what time you plan on leaving.

The app will automatically assume your current location is your starting point and the current time as your departure time unless you say otherwise.

## Seeing the Weather

Once routes are found, they will be displayed on the map along with weather information.

Below the map, you can see a summary of the trip, as well as the more detailed waypoint weather information.

You can select different route options to see how they compare.

## Updating a Trip

If you decide to change your origin, destination, or departure time, simply tap the magnifying glass to search again.

## Recents

Tap the clock button to see a list of trips you've planned before.

This can be helpful for reviewing preplanned, upcoming trips, as well as planning new trips with the same origin and destination.

## Opening in Apple Maps

Once you're ready for turn-by-turn directions, tap the turn arrow in the top right to open Apple Maps.
