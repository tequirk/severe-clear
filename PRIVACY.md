# Privacy Policy

This privacy policy applies to the Severe Clear app (hereby referred to as "Application") for mobile devices that was created by Tequirk LLC (hereby referred to as "Service Provider") as a Commercial service. This service is intended for use "AS IS".

## Information Collection and Use

The Application does not have user accounts, and the Service Provider does not operate a server that the Application sends your information to. The Service Provider does not collect your name, email address, contacts, or any other personally identifiable information, and the Application contains no analytics or advertising software.

Everything you enter into the Application — the places you search for, the trips you plan, and the times you plan them for — is stored only on your device.

If you download the Application from the Apple App Store, Apple provides the Service Provider with aggregate, anonymous statistics about downloads, crashes, and usage. The Service Provider cannot identify you from this information. Whether crash and usage data is shared with the Service Provider at all is controlled by you, in **Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements** on your device.

## Location

The Application asks for your location so that it can offer where you are now as the starting point for a trip. You can decline, or revoke it later in **Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services**, and continue using the Application by typing a starting point instead. The Application only requests location while you are using it, and never in the background.

To plan a trip, the Application sends locations to Apple in order to:

* find places matching what you type in the search field;
* calculate driving routes between your starting point and your destination;
* look up the names of towns and places along a route, so that waypoints can be labelled;
* retrieve the weather forecast for points along a route.

These locations are sent to Apple to answer those requests. They are not sent to the Service Provider, and the Service Provider does not receive, store, or have access to your location.

## Opt-Out Rights

You can stop the Application from using your location at any time in **Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services**, without losing the ability to plan trips.

You can remove everything the Application has stored by uninstalling it. You may use the standard uninstall processes as may be available as part of your mobile device or via the mobile application marketplace or network.

## Data Retention Policy

The Application stores two things on your device:

* **Your trip history** — the trips you have planned, kept so that you can review and replan them. This is your data. It is stored where your device's backups will include it, and it stays until you delete a trip from the history or uninstall the Application.
* **A cache of place names** — stored so that replanning a similar route does not have to look the same names up again. Your device may discard this at any time if it needs the space, and the Application will simply fetch them again.

Neither is sent anywhere. Because the Service Provider holds no data about you, there is nothing for them to delete on your behalf; uninstalling the Application removes all of it. If you have questions about this, contact them at legal@tequirk.com and they will respond in a reasonable time.
